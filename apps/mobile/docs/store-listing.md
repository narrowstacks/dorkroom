## Description

Dorkroom is a toolbox for analog photographers, in the darkroom and out in
the field.

- Light meter: meter the scene with the camera in aperture or shutter
  priority, with matrix or spot metering and film-log integration.
- Film log: track rolls, cameras, lenses and shots, with reference photos from
  the meter. Export your log.
- Development recipes: browse film and developer combinations from the
  Dorkroom database, with agitation patterns.
- Process timer: a multi-stage countdown for develop, stop, fix, wash and
  custom steps, with saved presets.
- Negative inverter: photograph a negative on a light table, or pick one from
  Photos, and get the positive, with film base detection, crop, rotate and
  flip, and exposure, contrast, warmth, tint and saturation adjustments.
- Border calculator: work out print and mat borders, including best fit.
- Exposure and reciprocity calculators: stops, thirds of a stop, and
  reciprocity compensation.
- Resize calculator for enlarger and print sizes.

Pin the tools you use most to the tab bar, jump straight into the meter,
border, exposure or reciprocity calculators from Home Screen quick actions,
and use Dorkroom links to open tools directly. Everything runs on your
device; the app is open source.

Requires iOS 26.
